## 1. The Interview

### Q1: Vibe in 3–5 words + non-web references
* **Vibe:** Cinematic Hyderabad Nocturne, Luminous Telemetry, Tactile Craft.
* **References:** *Blade Runner 2049* holographic lighting, *Coke Studio* cultural acoustic energy, and retro-futuristic astronomical planetarium maps.

### Q2: The scroll journey, section by section
1. **Act 0 (Orbit Entrance):** Hero live telemetry + 3D Hyderabad Constellation Orbit.
2. **Act 1 (Focus / Spotlight):** Curated gig & workshop spotlights with specular glare.
3. **Act 2 (Frequency Channels):** Sonic, Tactile Craft, Founder Mind, Stage Drama cards.
4. **Act 3 (The Living Wall):** Real-time event index floating on Morphing Rings.
5. **Act 4 (The Signal Engine):** Crawl → Deduplication → Constellation timeline.
6. **Act 5 (Horizon / Peak):** Deep obsidian cultural callout & Scraper trigger console.

### Q3: The energy curve
Calm & mysterious at the top → energetic & vibrant through the frequencies → calm & authoritative at the pipeline engine → climactic & radiant at the bottom.

### Q4: Feeling curve & The ONE moment
* **Feeling Curve:**
  * *Act 0:* Wonder & curiosity (floating illuminated Hyderabad coordinate mesh).
  * *Act 1:* Intimacy & focus (crisp event spotlights gliding into view).
  * *Act 2:* Exploration & agency (vibrant tactile frequency cards reacting to cursor/scroll).
  * *Act 3:* Abundance & clarity (a clean, deduplicated cultural wall over moving WebGL rings).
  * *Act 4:* Trust & provenance (transparent 3-step pipeline breakdown).
  * *Act 5:* Action & belonging (monumental dark horizon inviting deep exploration).
* **The Peak:** *"It's the site where the entire cultural canvas floats on a living, morphing WebGL universe that bends, rotates, and ripples as you scrub the wheel."* (Lives in Act 3 / Global Canvas).

### Q5: Signature Move
The 3D Hyderabad city orbit and full-viewport Morphing Rings dynamically synchronize camera pitch, tilt, and wave ripple amplitude to the user's thumb and wheel scroll velocity in real time.

### Q6: Aesthetic Family
*Dark Editorial / Cosmic Cultural Computing* — Deep obsidian ground (`#070A09`), frosted glassmorphic cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), luminous saffron (`#FF4800`), and emerald jade (`#00D084`) neon accents.

### Q7: World Structure
**One Unbroken World (Worldflight)** — The entire application feels like a single continuous deep-sky flight through Hyderabad's cultural coordinate space.

### Q8: Available Assets
Built-in Three.js constellation mesh, Originkit Morphing Rings WebGL engine, and live normalized event feeds.
